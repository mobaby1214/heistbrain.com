FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers.

How is Heist different from ChatGPT? +
ChatGPT gives you text and a blank page. Heist builds the system around it: brand memory that persists, platform-specific previews, campaign organization, and scheduling. It's the difference between a tool and a workflow.
How does the Brand Brain work? +
You define your voice, audience, and offer during onboarding. The Brain stores this permanently and applies it to every piece of content. The longer you use Heist, the more it sounds like you.
Does AI content sound robotic? +
Not when it has a Brain. Most AI tools are stateless, so they forget you after every session. Your Brain learns from you and applies your voice, tone, and style to every single post.
What platforms does Heist support? +
LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and WordPress: six platforms from one dashboard, plus a hosted blog on your own domain. YouTube supports native video uploads. X publishing connects through your own X developer app keys on a paid X API tier. TikTok, Pinterest, and Reddit are coming soon. Every plan also includes long-form blog generation in the web app, and through your AI chat on Pro, Founder, Agency and the trial.
Can I try it before I pay? +
Yes. Every new account gets a 7-day free trial of Pro: no credit card, no API key. Connect Heist to your own Claude (any Claude plan works, Free included) or ChatGPT (a paid plan with developer mode turned on), set up your Brain and see for yourself. Faceless video unlocks once you pick a paid plan.
What if I need more posts? +
There are no add-on post packs. If you hit your monthly limit, upgrade any time: Pro covers 500 posts a month, and Founder and Agency have no monthly cap. The limit counts every post you save in Heist, drafts included, per brand, and resets each calendar month.
What happens after my trial ends? +
Your content and Brain are saved, and you pick a plan to keep creating. You're never auto-charged; there's no card on file during the trial.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, cancel from Settings. No fees, no questions asked.
Do I own the content Heist creates? +
Absolutely. Everything generated is 100% yours.
What is Social SEO and why does it matter? +
Social SEO is the practice of optimizing your social media content so it ranks in platform search results, not just shows up in feeds. 46% of Gen Z prefers social media over traditional search engines (eMarketer, via Search Engine Land). TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube all have powerful internal search. Heist automatically writes keyword-optimized content for each platform's search algorithm, so your posts are discoverable long after they're published.
How does Heist optimize for Social SEO? +
The Brain analyzes your topic and identifies the keyword phrases your audience would actually search for on each platform. Then it places those keywords where each platform's algorithm looks: in the first sentence (LinkedIn, Instagram), in titles and tags (YouTube), and in the opening line (X). Every generated post includes a searchKeywords field showing exactly what was optimized for, so you can track what drives discovery.
Is there an API? +
Yes, through MCP. On Pro, Founder and Agency (and during the trial) you can create an API key in Settings and connect Cursor, Windsurf or any MCP client; Claude and ChatGPT connect with a sign-in instead. There's no general-purpose REST API for your own apps yet. Tell us what you'd build at support@heistbrain.com.
Can I manage multiple brands or clients? +
Yes. Founder ($99/mo) covers up to 5 Brand Brains and 3 team seats; Agency ($199/mo) covers up to 25 Brand Brains and 10 team seats. Each brand has its own voice, memory, and analytics, completely isolated. Both plans include a team workspace with role-based access (Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer) and content approval workflows.
Does Heist support video content? +
Yes, in several ways. Heist generates video for you: faceless videos from a script (paid plans; AI voiceover plus stock or AI-generated visuals, sized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts), plus AI images (on your own OpenAI, Google or Replicate key) and AI video clips (on your own Replicate key) to attach to your posts; X publishes text-only posts and Instagram publishes single-image posts. It all auto-drafts to your calendar, ready to schedule. You can also upload your own videos directly to YouTube with chunked, resumable uploads; TikTok upload is coming soon. Either way, the Brain writes optimized captions, descriptions, tags, and hashtags for each platform.
What is a faceless video and how does Heist make one? +
A faceless video is short-form content that tells a story without you on camera: narrated visuals instead of a talking head. From one script, Heist generates an AI voiceover (ElevenLabs), pulls stock footage or generates on-brand images, and renders it into a captioned slideshow sized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts, typically about 60 seconds. It lands in your library and as a draft, ready to caption and schedule. Faceless video generation uses your own ElevenLabs and Creatomate API keys.
How does the auto-learning work? +
Every 6 hours, Heist pulls engagement data from your connected platforms. Posts that outperform your average by 2x are automatically identified, and the patterns that made them work are added to your Brain as insight facts. Your next generation is informed by what actually performed, not guesswork.
Is my content safe? Can I undo mistakes? +
Yes on both. Deleted posts go to Trash and can be restored for 60 days. Blog posts skip Trash, so deleting one is permanent (Heist asks first). Every edit saves the previous version, so you can revert to any point in history. Drafts save automatically as you type. Your database is backed up automatically every day, with integrity verification. And platform tokens, API keys and integration credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
