FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers.

How is Heist different from ChatGPT? +
ChatGPT gives you text and a blank page. Heist builds the system around it: brand memory that persists, platform-specific previews, campaign organization, and scheduling. It's the difference between a tool and a workflow.
How does the Brand Brain work? +
You define your voice, audience, and offer during onboarding. The Brain stores this permanently and applies it to every piece of content. The longer you use Heist, the more it sounds like you.
Does AI content sound robotic? +
Not when it has a Brain. Most AI tools are stateless, so they forget you after every session. Your Brain learns from you and applies your voice, tone, and style to every single post.
What platforms does Heist support? +
LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and WordPress: six platforms from one dashboard, plus a hosted blog on your own domain. YouTube supports native video uploads. X publishing connects through your own X developer app keys on a paid X API tier. TikTok, Pinterest, and Reddit are coming soon. The Founder and Agency plans also include blog content generation.
Can I try it before I pay? +
Yes. Every plan includes a 7-day free trial with full Pro features, and your first 25 posts are generated on us. No credit card, no API key. Set up your Brain and see for yourself.
What if I need more posts? +
You can purchase generation credit packs starting at $9 for 50 extra posts, or upgrade your plan at any time. No long-term commitments.
What happens after my trial ends? +
Your content and Brain are saved, and you pick a plan to keep creating. You're never auto-charged; there's no card on file during the trial.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, cancel from Settings. No fees, no questions asked.
Do I own the content Heist creates? +
Absolutely. Everything generated is 100% yours.
What is Social SEO and why does it matter? +
Social SEO is the practice of optimizing your social media content so it ranks in platform search results, not just shows up in feeds. 46% of Gen Z prefers social media over traditional search engines (eMarketer, via Search Engine Land). TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube all have powerful internal search. Heist automatically writes keyword-optimized content for each platform's search algorithm, so your posts are discoverable long after they're published.
How does Heist optimize for Social SEO? +
The Brain analyzes your topic and identifies the keyword phrases your audience would actually search for on each platform. Then it places those keywords where each platform's algorithm looks: in the first sentence (LinkedIn, Instagram), in titles and tags (YouTube), and in the opening line (X). Every generated post includes a searchKeywords field showing exactly what was optimized for, so you can track what drives discovery.
Is there an API? +
Not yet, but it's on our roadmap. Let us know at support@heistbrain.com.
Can I manage multiple brands or clients? +
Yes. The Agency plan ($199/mo) supports up to 25 Brand Brains, each with its own voice, memory, and analytics, completely isolated. You also get 10 team seats with role-based access (Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer) and content approval workflows.
Does Heist support video content? +
Yes, in several ways. Heist generates video for you: faceless videos from a script (AI voiceover plus stock or AI-generated visuals, sized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts), plus AI images and AI video clips you can attach to a post on any of the six platforms. It all auto-drafts to your calendar, ready to schedule. You can also upload your own videos directly to YouTube with chunked, resumable uploads; TikTok upload is coming soon. Either way, the Brain writes optimized captions, descriptions, tags, and hashtags for each platform.
What is a faceless video and how does Heist make one? +
A faceless video is short-form content that tells a story without you on camera: narrated visuals instead of a talking head. From one script, Heist generates an AI voiceover (ElevenLabs), pulls stock footage or generates on-brand images, and renders it into a captioned slideshow sized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts, typically about 60 seconds. It lands in your library and as a draft, ready to caption and schedule. Faceless video generation uses your own ElevenLabs and Creatomate API keys.
How does the auto-learning work? +
Every 6 hours, Heist pulls engagement data from your connected platforms (Meta, X, LinkedIn). Posts that outperform your average by 2x are automatically identified, and the patterns that made them work are added to your Brain as insight facts. Your next generation is informed by what actually performed, not guesswork.
Is my content safe? Can I undo mistakes? +
Yes on both. Deleted content goes to Trash and is recoverable for 30 days. Every edit saves the previous version, so you can revert to any point in history. Drafts autosave every 30 seconds. Your database is backed up every 6 hours with integrity verification. And all data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
