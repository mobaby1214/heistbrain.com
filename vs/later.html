The short version

Later is one of the best visual content schedulers on the market, especially for Instagram. It has a visual grid preview, native Stories + Reels support, a Linkin.bio tool, and deep Instagram-specific analytics. If you're a visual-first creator or brand, Later's UX is purpose-built for you.

Heist is a full content operating system — generation, scheduling, and analytics in one. It has a Brand Brain that generates platform-native content from your voice and past performance, then schedules it. Instead of "bring your own content," Heist creates the content.

The question: do you already have the content and just need scheduling, or do you want a tool that generates AND schedules?

Where Later is better

  • Visual scheduling UX. Later's visual calendar is genuinely best-in-class. Drag-and-drop, grid preview, asset library — all refined for Instagram-first workflows.
  • Instagram Stories + Reels depth. Native support for scheduling Stories with cover frames and Reels with custom covers. This is where Later's Instagram focus shows.
  • Linkin.bio. Bundled link-in-bio tool that matches your Instagram grid. A nice-to-have if you run Instagram as a primary channel.
  • Pinterest. Later supports Pinterest scheduling. Heist doesn't.
  • Established team features. Later has been a multi-user team tool for years. Approval workflows and client management are mature.

Where Heist wins

  • AI generation, not just AI captions. Later has basic caption suggestions. Heist has a Brand Brain that generates full, platform-native posts from your voice, audience, and past performance. Different category of tool.
  • Content creation, not just scheduling. Later assumes you bring your own content. Heist creates it for you. If "what do I post today" is a recurring problem, that difference matters.
  • Performance feedback loop. Heist learns from your actual engagement and uses that in new generations. Later shows you the data but doesn't close the loop.
  • Long-form content. Heist generates SEO blog posts in your brand voice on every plan. Later is social-only.
  • One workflow instead of two. Later (from $18.75, billed annually) + ChatGPT Plus ($20) ≈ $39-45/mo with copy-paste in between. Heist Pro ($49) plugs into that same ChatGPT plan (with developer mode turned on) or into Claude, and does the scheduling, previews and brand memory in one place — it costs more, but it skips the copy-paste.

Who should pick which

Pick Later if: you're a visual-first creator, Instagram is your primary channel, you post a lot of Stories and Reels, or you need Pinterest scheduling.

Pick Heist if: you post text-first content across multiple platforms, you want AI to generate the content (not just schedule it), or you want one tool for brand memory + generation + scheduling + analytics.

Where Later is better

  • Visual-first scheduling UX. Later was built for Instagram and you can feel it. The visual grid preview, drag-and-drop calendar, and asset library are genuinely best-in-class for visual content.
  • Instagram Stories + Reels support. Later has native Stories scheduling with full cover-frame preview and Reels scheduling with cover customization. Heist doesn't publish Stories or Reels; on Instagram it posts single-image feed posts.
  • Link in Bio tool. Linkin.bio is a legitimately useful link-in-bio product bundled with Later. Heist doesn't have an equivalent.
  • Pinterest support. Later has full Pinterest scheduling. Heist doesn't cover Pinterest.
  • Team collaboration maturity. Later has deeper team roles, approval workflows, and client collaboration than Heist does today.

Where Heist is better

  • AI content generation with brand memory. Later has basic AI caption suggestions. Heist generates full posts with a Brand Brain that knows your voice, audience, and past performance.
  • Platform-native generation. Heist generates posts that are already formatted for each platform (LinkedIn essay-length, X punchline, Instagram caption). Later expects you to bring your own content.
  • Performance learning loop. Heist's Brain ingests engagement data and uses it in new generations. Later shows you analytics but doesn't close the loop back into generation.
  • Long-form blog support. Heist generates SEO blog posts in the same voice as your social content, on every plan. Later is social-only.
  • No copy-paste between AI and scheduler. Later + ChatGPT Plus ≈ $39-45/mo, and you're still pasting. Heist Pro ($49/mo, plus your own AI plan or key) does both in one workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Is Heist a Later alternative?

For text-first content across LinkedIn, X, Facebook, and YouTube, yes. For visual-first Instagram scheduling with Stories, Reels, and Linkin.bio, Later is probably still the better fit.

Does Heist support Instagram Stories and Reels?

Not yet. Heist schedules single-image Instagram feed posts; it doesn't publish Stories or Reels. If those are core to your Instagram, Later is the better fit.

Can Heist replace Later for Pinterest?

Not yet — Pinterest support in Heist is coming soon. If Pinterest is a core channel for you today, keep Later (or use both tools together).
