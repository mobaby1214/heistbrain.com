Why coaches struggle with content

Coaching is a trust business. The content you post is often the only thing a future client sees before booking a call — which means your social presence IS your marketing funnel. But when you're one person running a coaching practice, content creation is the thing that always slips. Heist is built for that exact problem.

Three pains, three fixes

Client work eats all your time

Every hour in a session is an hour you're not marketing. By the time clients are done, you're too drained to write three posts and schedule them across platforms.

Generic AI sounds generic

ChatGPT gives you content that could be any coach's. Your audience can tell. You need content in your specific voice with your specific philosophy — not templated wisdom.

Consistency is make-or-break

Prospects check your feed before booking. A dead account = dead funnel. Missing even a week of posts costs real leads.

How Heist fixes each one

Fix 1: A Brain that remembers your coaching philosophy

Set up your Brain once with your coaching framework, your philosophy, your ideal client, your proof, and 3-5 examples of your best past content. From that moment on, every post Heist generates pulls from all of that. You never re-explain what you teach or who you help.

Fix 2: Weekly batching in one sitting

Pick a theme (common coaching challenge, client win, or insight from your week). List 5-7 angles. Hit generate. Heist drafts a full week of content — LinkedIn essays, Instagram posts, X posts — all in your voice. Add an image to each Instagram post, drag to calendar, schedule. Done. (Instagram publishes single-image posts; X runs on your own X developer keys and a paid X API tier.) Here's the full workflow.

Fix 3: Content that positions you as the expert

Heist's Brain uses the hook frameworks that actually stop the scroll — contrarian takes, specific numbers, confessions from client work. Every post is positioned to demonstrate expertise, not just "motivate." The difference between "you got this" and "here's the specific reframe I use with clients in week 3" is the difference between 10 likes and a booked call.

Plays coaches run with Heist

  • The case study play. Every client win becomes a 3-post mini-campaign: the problem, the turning point, the result (anonymized as needed). Heist generates the sequence from three bullet points you dictate.
  • The daily insight play. Pick one hard-won lesson from your own practice per week. Heist turns it into 5 posts across different platforms — same core insight, different angles.
  • The launch play. New program, new workshop, or open spots? Heist generates a launch sequence: tease, insight, proof, offer, close. 10-15 posts organized as a campaign.
  • The FAQ play. Every question you get asked repeatedly becomes a post. Heist turns "how do I know if I need a coach?" into a LinkedIn post, an X post, and an Instagram post — all from one prompt.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best content tool for coaches?

For solo coaches and small coaching practices, you need AI generation, brand memory, and scheduling in one tool — not four. Heist Pro at $49/mo (plus your own AI plan or key) covers all of that. For coaches who only post on one platform and already have a scheduler, simpler tools like Buffer can work.

How can a solo coach post consistently without burning out?

Batch a week of content in one sitting once per week. That's the only way to sustain daily posting as a one-person operation. Discipline doesn't scale, systems do. Set up your Brain once, then every batching session is fast because the AI already knows your voice.

Will AI-generated content sound generic?

Generic AI does. Heist's 10-layer Brain stores your philosophy, your framework, your past content, and your voice examples — so generations sound like you, not a template. The difference is night and day after 2-3 generations when the Brain locks in.

What's the best platform for coaches in 2026?

LinkedIn for B2B coaches (executive, leadership, business). Instagram for life coaches, health, relationships. TikTok for coaches targeting under-35 audiences. X for thought-leadership in specific niches. Heist posts to LinkedIn, Instagram and X from one Brain without rewriting content four times (X runs on your own X developer keys and a paid X API tier). It writes your TikTok scripts too, but TikTok publishing isn't live yet, so you post those yourself.
