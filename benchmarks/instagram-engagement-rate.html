Average Instagram Engagement Rate (2026)

The real numbers on Instagram engagement — sourced from Rival IQ and Socialinsider benchmark studies. Figures checked September 2026.

The short answer

The median brand account earns 0.36% engagement per follower; the top quartile clears 1.02%. Rival IQ's 2025 Social Media Industry Benchmark Report puts the median Instagram engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, and reactions divided by followers) at 0.36% across all industries, with the top 25% of brands at 1.02% or higher. Socialinsider's separate study of 35 million posts measured a 0.48% average for brand pages in 2025 — and engagement is falling on both datasets (Rival IQ measured a 16% year-over-year decline).

How to calculate your Instagram engagement rate

Engagement rate = (likes + comments + saves + shares) / total followers × 100. Some definitions also include impressions, but the simpler formula is standard for benchmarking.

What counts as "good" engagement on Instagram in 2026?

Use the sourced quartiles rather than guru numbers:

  • At or above 0.36%: you're at or above the all-industry median for brand accounts (Rival IQ, 2025).
  • Above roughly 1%: you're in the top quartile — Rival IQ puts the top 25% of brands at 1.02% or higher.
  • Well below 0.36%: compare against your own industry's median first (industries range from under 0.2% to over 2%) before assuming your content is the problem.

Personal and creator accounts often run higher than brand pages — the studies above measure brands — so treat these as a floor, not a ceiling, if you're a creator.

Why engagement rates keep declining

Rival IQ measured Instagram engagement down 16% year over year in its 2025 report, and Socialinsider's data shows a similar slide. Three factors have driven the decline:

  1. Feed saturation. There's more content than attention. Every year the feed gets more crowded.
  2. Algorithm prioritization of Reels. Instagram has been pushing Reels hard since 2022, and Reels engagement happens in a different pattern than feed engagement. Many creators see their total engagement rate drop even as their total reach grows.
  3. Audience behavior shifts. Younger users have moved to TikTok. Older users are less engagement-forward (fewer comments, more lurking).

What moves engagement rate up

  • Lean on carousels and Reels over single images. In Socialinsider's 2025 data, carousels (0.55%) and Reels (0.52%) both clearly out-engage single images (0.37%), and carousels lead on saves and views per post.
  • Use carousels for educational content. Save-worthy content is what carousels do best in the format data.
  • Ask specific questions. "What's your experience?" drives more comments than "what do you think?"
  • Post at the right times. See our Instagram timing guide for optimal windows by industry.
  • Write hooks that earn the tap. The first line or two of your caption decides whether someone hits "more" or scrolls past. Our hook generator has frameworks specifically for Instagram captions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good engagement rate on Instagram in 2026?

Benchmark against the median, not a guru number. Rival IQ's 2025 benchmark report puts the median Instagram engagement rate at 0.36% of followers across all industries, and the top 25% of brands clear 1.02%. If your rate is above roughly 1%, you're in the top quartile of brand accounts.

Why is my Instagram engagement rate so low?

Most likely causes: follower-content mismatch (your audience doesn't care about your posts), weak hooks that don't earn the tap, or a format mix that leans on static images — Socialinsider's 2025 data shows images engaging at 0.37% vs 0.55% for carousels and 0.52% for Reels. Fix the hooks and rebalance your formats first.

How do I calculate my Instagram engagement rate?

Add your likes + comments + saves + shares, divide by your follower count, multiply by 100. Or use our free engagement rate calculator which handles the math and compares to industry benchmarks.
